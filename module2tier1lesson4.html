Lesson 4

Basic Lifesaving Skills

First hours matter. Simple skills save lives.

Essential Skills (click to expand)
  • First Aid & Bandaging: clean wounds, control bleeding, treat burns/fractures.
  • Choking Response: safe abdominal thrusts for adults/children.
  • CPR: chest compressions and rescue breaths for cardiac arrest.
  • Flood Safety: avoid currents, cut electricity, move to high ground, use ropes/floatation.

Family & Church Drills

  • Household evacuation and meet-up plans; “Go Bag” practice.
  • Church-wide fire/quake/flood simulations with barangay partners.
  • Convert knowledge into muscle memory to reduce panic.
Safety first: brief volunteers; use PPE; never send untrained persons into hazardous zones.
