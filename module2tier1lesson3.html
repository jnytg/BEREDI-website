Lesson 3

Emotional & Spiritual Care

Disasters wound the body and the soul. The Church’s ministry of presence brings peace.

Prayer, Listening, Pastoral Counseling

  • Pray simply and sincerely; invite God’s comfort.
  • Listen without judgment; allow grief and lament.
  • Counsel with Scripture and hope; remind of God’s nearness.

Share Hope Through Presence

Show up. Sit beside. Help carry debris. Offer a verse or song. Presence itself communicates, “God sees you.”

Evangelism that Flows from Compassion

Answer “Why are you helping?” with testimony: “Because Christ loves us—and you.” Use prayer stations, Bible distribution, short devos—always respectful and voluntary.

Reflect: How will your team integrate prayer and counseling into relief operations?
