Programs & Training

We equip churches and communities with essential skills to effectively respond to disasters. Our training program is divided into four levels, each focusing on a key stage of disaster risk reduction.

Readiness

Prepare individuals and communities with disaster awareness, risk assessment, and preparedness strategies before an emergency occurs.

Response

Train volunteers and leaders to act quickly and effectively during emergencies to ensure safety, rescue, and immediate support.

Recovery

Provide skills for post-disaster recovery, rehabilitation, and rebuilding efforts to restore normalcy in affected communities.

Mitigation / Prevention

Empower communities to minimize future risks through sustainable practices and infrastructure improvements.
