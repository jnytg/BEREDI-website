About BEREDI

BEREDI is a faith-driven, church-based movement committed to preparing and mobilizing churches to respond to disasters with compassion and coordination.

We aim to:

  • Strengthen church-based disaster preparedness systems.

  • Train volunteers in emergency response and coordination.

  • Promote community resilience through education and planning.

  • Build partnerships with local and global organizations.

  • We are committed to transforming churches into active agents of safety, compassion, and restoration.

    • Our Core Focus Areas

    Readiness

  • We conduct preparedness training, risk assessments, and resource planning to ensure churches are ready before disasters happen.

    • Response

  • We mobilize volunteers, distribute relief goods, coordinate evacuation efforts, and provide spiritual and emotional care during crises.

    • Recovery

  • We support rebuilding efforts, community rehabilitation, and trauma care to help families restore stability.

    • Mitigation & Prevention

  • We educate communities on risk reduction strategies and sustainable practices to minimize disaster impact.

    • Our Team Projects

    BEREDI’s work is driven by dedicated volunteers, church leaders, and disaster response coordinators who lead impactful projects such as:

  • Community Disaster Preparedness Workshops
  • Emergency Response Simulations
  • Church-Based Relief Distribution Systems
  • Volunteer Training Programs
  • Partner Mapping & Coordination Initiatives
  • Resource Mobilization Campaigns

    • We envision a global network of churches equipped and united in disaster response—serving as beacons of hope before, during, and after emergencies.

    Each project is designed to strengthen local churches and improve coordinated disaster action at the grassroots level.
