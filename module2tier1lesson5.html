Lesson 5

Volunteerism & Commitment

Serving in crisis is worship in action. Every believer has a role.

Personal Readiness to Serve Neighbors

  • Be Prepared: hazard awareness, home plan, supplies.
  • Be Available: time, skills, presence.
  • Be Teachable: trainings, drills, debriefs.
  • Be Compassionate: humility and dignity for all.

Mindset: “The Church Is a First Responder in Christ’s Love”

  • From spectator to servant.
  • From comfort zone to compassion zone.
  • From reaction to readiness.
Challenge: What concrete commitment will you make this week to strengthen your church’s response?
