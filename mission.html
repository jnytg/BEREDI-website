Mission & Vision

Guided by faith, compassion, and preparedness in service to communities.

Our Mission

To equip churches and communities to become frontline responders in times of disaster by providing training, tools, and a biblical foundation for compassion-led action.

Our Vision

A global church network united and prepared for disaster response — bringing hope, healing, and restoration to affected communities.

What We Believe

  • • Faith as the foundation for action
  • • Empowerment of local churches
  • • Compassionate and sustainable responses
  • • Global collaboration for preparedness
